## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is CoH2: Defiance?
**A:** CoH2: Defiance is a total overhaul mod built on Company of Heroes 2, designed to offer a fresh but familiar experience. Our goal is simple: a version of CoH2 where every match is decided by strategy and skill rather than broken mechanics or lopsided matchups. The transition from vanilla CoH2 to Defiance is intentionally smooth, players can dive in without relearning the entire game.

### Q: How does Defiance change the base game?
**A:** Defiance builds on the core mechanics of Company of Heroes 2 while introducing new units, abilities, and refined gameplay systems. Weapon damage, cover interactions, and explosive effects have been made more consistent and intuitive, all while staying true to the vanilla feel. For those interested in the full technical breakdown, a detailed document covering the deeper balance and mechanical changes will be made available.

### Q: Are there new units or abilities?
**A:** Yes. Defiance introduces a standalone doctrine system that replaces the vanilla commander model. Unlike the paid DLC commanders in the base game, all doctrines in Defiance are available to everyone from the start. Each doctrine comes with its own unique units, upgrades, and abilities, giving players the tools to adapt their strategy and counter their opponents in new ways.

### Q: Will this mod include experimental or prototype weapons?
**A:** Selectively, yes. Defiance is set in an alternate 1946-1947 timeline, which provides a narrative framework for late-war prototypes, experimental designs, and certain fictional concepts to appear alongside historical equipment. These additions exist to create new and engaging gameplay mechanics rather than to tell a historical story, and are kept restrained compared to other workshop mods. Don't expect science fiction, but the occasional unconventional design is very much on the table.

### Q: Will this mod be compatible with other mods?
**A:** By design, CoH2: Defiance is a tuning pack, meaning it won't be compatible with other tuning packs. However, it doesn't alter core game modes, so win conditions and custom scenarios should remain usable. Custom skin packs are also fully supported, so you're free to use any skins you like alongside Defiance.

### Q: Is this mod balanced for competitive play, or more for a casual audience?
**A:** Defiance is not designed with competitive play as its primary focus. Our goal is fun, consistency, and reducing the frustrating mechanics that made vanilla CoH2 feel unfair at times. Elements like weapon slot drops, random vehicle crits, and overly RNG-heavy explosive damage have been streamlined or removed entirely. That said, the mod is designed with a high skill ceiling in mind, there are soft counters to almost everything, and the better your understanding of the factions and their tools, the more options you'll have on the battlefield. Think of it less like a competitive ladder and more like a well tuned sandbox where skill and strategy determine the outcome. Arranged competitive matches are possible, but that's not who we built this for.

### Q: How many custom doctrines will we see in Defiance?
**A:** Defiance will launch with 4 doctrines per faction, each offering its own unique playstyle, units, and abilities. Following release, we plan to add more doctrines as post-launch content, all of which will be completely free of charge. Follow our official Discord for updates on post-launch content as it develops.

### Q: Do you plan to add [X features] in future updates?
**A:** We're always open to good ideas, and if something fits our vision for Defiance it might make its way in. That said, we have a clear direction for the mod and not every suggestion will be implemented. If it's interesting and enhances the gameplay without compromising balance or consistency, it's worth a conversation. Follow our official Discord to stay up to date and join the discussion.

### Q: Why does Defiance have less content compared to other mods or even vanilla CoH2?
**A**: Our team is small, and our priority is delivering a polished and balanced experience rather than flooding the game with new units for the sake of numbers. Content bloat often dilutes gameplay, so we've chosen to focus on refining the mechanics and units we already have. That said, we do plan to release additional doctrines and content post-launch, all free of charge. Follow our official Discord for progress updates, previews, and discussions about future additions.

### Q: Does the mod have faction bias?
**A**: We'll be honest: most of our dev team mains Allies. That said, we believe that to create a strong protagonist, you also need an equally strong antagonist. Every faction in Defiance has been designed to stand on its own, with unique strengths and tools that reward players who take the time to learn them. At the end of the day, it's your skill and strategy that determines victory, not developer bias.

### Q: Where are all the Soviet Guards units?
**A**: Honest answer: we didn't enjoy how Guards were designed and balanced in vanilla CoH2, so we made the call to hold off on them for launch. That said, Guards aren't gone forever, they already exist in the mod files and will make their return as post-launch content. Follow our official Discord for updates on when they'll be making their comeback.
